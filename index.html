    How it works

    Simple helpdesk workflow

    1. 1
      Submit

      A customer raises a request with details and optional files.

    2. 2
      Review

      Agents see new, in progress, and waiting requests in the Open inbox.

    3. 3
      Track

      Requesters can track their cases, see customer-facing updates, and reply.

    4. 4
      Resolve

      Agents add resolution notes. Resolved cases close automatically after 14 days.
